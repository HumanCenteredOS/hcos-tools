## Prompts

HCOS™ professional prompts provide structured instructions for applying
human-centered systems thinking to professional work, evidence, decisions,
communication, workflows, and organizational learning.

- [HCOS™ Workflow Discovery and Instrument Routing Prompt](Prompts/HCOS_Workflow_Discovery_and_Instrument_Routing_Prompt.md)  
  Reviews historical emails, notes, and workflow evidence to reconstruct
  how work occurred, identify human load and enduring systems lessons, and
  determine which HCOS™ instrument or other artifact may provide support.
